A production lab

Mila, a 37-year-old director, and Paulo, a 54-year-old shop owner, guide six fictional cases. You create a first version of each case and choose one to finish. Deliver six studies in MP4, one revised final version, and organized source files.

Open the scripts, sheets, and study track · View Codex teaching references.

The images explain decisions; they are not frames from rendered campaigns. The kit contains scripts, references, and audio. You create the clips and ads during the activities.

Before you begin

You should know how to generate an image, save a clip, and make basic edits in an editor. If you have not done that yet, start with Image Prompting, Kling AI, and CapCut. This lab brings those skills together.

Use a computer, image generator, video generator, and editor. Check available credits, plans, and features before production. External tools and queues are not included. The ten-minute practice is only one part; full production takes additional time.

The cases do not promise sales, reach, product performance, or a finished ad in ten minutes. Label the fictional demonstrations. A step remains pending until you have the required file.

Your progress stays in this browser. Save a copy along the way. The course includes reading, examples, and activities; it has no prerecorded video lessons.
